How do I buy a vehicle? +

Contact me through WhatsApp or the inquiry form. Tell me the vehicle you are looking for and your destination. I will guide you through the selection and export process.

How much do I pay initially? +

The standard process described on this website uses a 50% advance payment, with the remaining amount handled according to the agreed transaction terms.

Can I track my vehicle? +

Yes. Shipping documentation and tracking information can be shared during the shipment process.

When do I pay the remaining amount? +

The remaining payment is handled according to the agreed transaction schedule. Confirm the exact timing before ordering.

How are original documents delivered? +

After the required final payment, original shipping documents can be sent by courier such as FedEx.

Do you also provide auto parts? +

Yes. I can help customers source Japanese automotive parts according to their requirements.
